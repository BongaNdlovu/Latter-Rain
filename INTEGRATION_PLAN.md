# Rain of the Word: V2 integration plan

The source is the single-file V2 game (`skate_game_v2.html`). The target is this modular project, which uses the `window.ROW` namespace and loads `data → core → fx → audio → env → ground → weather → rain → storm → disasters → entities → board → camera → main`.

Rules:
- Never downgrade a system where the project is already stronger.
- Keep every project-only feature: disasters, the tsunami every 10th level, the verse/collection loop, medkits, bombs, checkpoints, Focus, photo mode, stress suite, hit-stop, whip-pan and chromatic aberration.
- Add new code as new modules, or as small hooks inside the module that owns that concern.

Verdicts: **P** = project's version is better, keep it. **V** = V2's is better or the project has none, port it. **M** = merge both.

| # | V2 feature | Project has? | Verdict | Action / module |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **3D board renderer** (`drawSkateboard`: true 3D deck/trucks/wheels with flip, spin and pitch) | 2D side-profile deck with trucks; cannot flip or spin | **V** (worse in project) | New `js/board3d.js` (V2 renderer, unchanged). `board.js` `B.draw` swaps the 2D deck and trucks for `drawSkateboard`. It **keeps** the project's contact shadow, aura (streak rainbow, damage red, Transformer blaze), squash, lean, bPulse rim glow, Lamp halo, hex **shield dome**, Transformer embers, wheel spray and the reflection pass. Deck graphic = the board's `art` colours; the Transformer gets a molten deck. |
| 2 | Tricks: kickflip, heelflip, 360 shove-it, hardflip combo; combos with banking; manuals; bail | Jump only (W/↑) | **V** | New `js/tricks.js` (`R.tricks`), hooked into `board.js` jump/land. Keys go on free keys only: **J/Z** kick, **K/X** heel, **L/C** shove, two of them together = hardflip, hold **S/↓** = manual. Space stays Focus; W/↑ stays jump; P, O and M are untouched. A trick buffered just before the jump fires right after take-off. A bail costs the combo and staggers the board but not a heart. Clearing project debris mid-air adds a "Clear" link to the combo. A touch ✦ button triggers a kickflip. |
| 3 | Rolling obstacles (rollers) | Scrolling road debris per ground type, two-zone collision, "Clean!" score, crash knockback | **M** (project system better) | Keep the project's debris. Add one **rolling tyre** type in `ground.js`: it rolls faster than the road, uses the same collision and scoring, and is drawn spinning on every ground. |
| 4 | XP, ranks, deck unlocks | Board unlocks by level/verses/score/card; no XP or ranks | **M** | XP and the 6 ranks (Grom → Legend) live in `tricks.js`. XP comes from tricks, manuals, debris clears and finished verses, saved in `rotw-xp`. A rank HUD and XP bar sit under the score. Two **XP-gated boards** are added (Aurora Veil 25k, Midnight Chrome 60k) with `req.kind:'xp'` in `M.boardUnlocked`. A saved Transformer index is migrated so the selection survives. The existing achievement unlocks are unchanged. |
| 5 | Cinematic: title cards, camera, letterbox, slow-mo | `#trans` travel card, letterbox `R.lbH`, whip-pan, punch, hit-stop, CA, handheld camera, Focus slow-mo | **P** + small **V** additions | Keep the project camera and letterbox. Add: (a) the `#trans` title card also at **run start** (location + weather); (b) a **big-trick slow-mo**: on a multi-trick, hardflip or 4+ combo landing the world slows (`R.slowF`), the letterbox opens wider, the camera punches and the CA pulses, plus a banner ("Sick Combo", "Legendary Line", "Bail!"). |
| 6 | Locations: 4 fantasy (Rooftop Bowl, Midnight Harbor, Lantern Temple, Canyon Highway) and 10 real cities | 11 places, including London, Paris, Manhattan and Tokyo with animated landmarks (rotating Eye, beacon, torch, clock glow) | **M** | London, Paris, New York and Tokyo: the project's versions are richer (animated), so **keep them**. Port the 4 fantasy places and the 6 missing cities (Rio, Cape Town, Sydney, Dubai, Rome, Jerusalem) with their landmarks into new `js/world.js`. It registers new skyline generators through `R.env.GEN` / `R.env.PAL`, which `env.js` now exposes. Appended to `R.LOC` as indices 11–20, so stress-test indices are unchanged. |
| 7 | Realistic weather: depth rain, ripples, fog banks, heat haze, snow sprites, snow accumulation, 3 cloud layers, branching lightning, rolling thunder, wind | Rain strata, rain ticks, gusts, lens drops, hail, sand, distance-delayed thunder, sheet lightning, god rays, 10 types | **M** | Rain, storm, thunder and hail: the project's are as good or better, so **keep them**. Port the pieces it lacks into `weather.js`: drifting **fog banks** (back and front), **heat haze** shimmer (Dubai, Canyon, Judean Desert), **snow accumulation** (drawn on the ground after `ground.draw`). In `rain.js`, replace the hard-edged arc snowflakes with V2's **soft sprites**. Add 3 daylight types for the new places in `data.js`: **Sunlit, Golden Dusk, Heatwave** (`heat` key). |
| 8 | Location picker + World Tour starting in London | Fixed linear order starting at Sea of Galilee | **V** | `main.js`: a menu `<select>` with World Tour, Random, or any of the 21 places, saved in `rotw-start`. **World Tour** follows `R.TOUR` (London first, interleaving cities, fantasy places and the original places). Level → location now goes through `M.locFor(lvl)`, so `pickWeather`, checkpoints and travel all agree. |
| 9 | Perf caching (reduced-res copy for haze, sprite caches, skipping off-screen copies) | Q fallback, cached skyline layers, stress suite | **M** | The new effects use caches from the start: one fog texture, one flake sprite, a half-height strip copy for haze, all skipped when `R.Q<1`. The 3D board's polygon cache (`SBC`) comes with `board3d.js`. |
| 10 | Rain index crash fix (`drops[i]` past the end) | `rain.js` already clamps: `n=floor(len*min(1,rain/1.3)*Q)` | **P / N/A** | Not needed. Verified the project's index can never exceed `drops.length`. |
| 11 | HUD label refresh on place change | `renderVerse()` refreshes the location every verse | **P** | Keep. |

Files touched:
- New: `js/board3d.js`, `js/tricks.js`, `js/world.js`
